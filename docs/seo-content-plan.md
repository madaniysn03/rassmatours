# Rassma Tours — Content gap report and editorial plan

Companion to `seo-keyword-map.md`. Everything here is aligned with the services actually published by the company (five tours, tailor-made days, transfers, multi-day circuits, ferry assistance). Nothing should be published until the "verify" items are confirmed by the client.

## 1. Pages that need additional content

| Page | Current state | What is missing | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/tours/tangier-food-tour/` | Thinnest tour page: the source site gives a one-line description and three highlights; the rest is editorial. | Number of stops, examples of dishes actually tasted, duration of tastings, whether lunch is included, photos from the tour. | High |

## 2. Information to request from the client

1. Relationship between **Rassma Tours** and **Tangier By Guide** (brand change, two brands, acquisition) and which domain will be used.
2. Logo, brand colours if any, and the official spelling of the name.
3. Founding year, team, guide licences, insurance, the TripAdvisor listing to be linked (the existing one is titled "Tangier Discovery Tours").
4. For each tour: confirmed itinerary order, start times, meeting point, inclusions/exclusions, entrance fees, price or price range, deposit and cancellation policy, group size limits, accessibility.
5. Original photographs (tours, guides, vehicles, guests with consent) to replace the Unsplash placeholders.
6. Authorisation for the three testimonials; links to Google Business Profile and social accounts (currently none verified).
7. Which languages to publish next (the existing site had Spanish; the brief mentions French).
8. Legal notice data and the email service to connect to the inquiry form.

## 3. Long-tail topics worth a dedicated section or FAQ (not new pages yet)

These answer real questions found in the search results and in marketplace listings. Add them to existing pages first; only create new pages when there is enough original material.

- "Tangier day trip from Tarifa: how the ferry timing works" (contact page or Tangier destination page).
- "Tangier cruise excursion: what fits in a 6-hour port call" (Cap Spartel tour page).
- "How far is Chefchaouen from Tangier and is it worth the drive" (already on the Chefchaouen destination page; expand with a sample timeline once confirmed).
- "Cap Spartel vs Caves of Hercules: can you see both in a morning" (Cap Spartel tour page).
- "What to wear in the medina / Ramadan opening hours / Friday market closures" (general FAQ, needs guide input).
- "Is a private guide worth it in Tangier" (About page or a short editorial).

## 4. FAQ opportunities

Implemented: six general FAQs (booking, languages, payment, customisation, private tours, ferry), three per tour, two to three per destination. Candidates once verified: cancellation policy, deposit amount, child pricing, maximum group size, wheelchair accessibility, pickup from Tanger Med vs the city port, tipping customs.

Note: FAQ structured data (`FAQPage`) was deliberately **not** added. Since 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government and health sites, so the markup would add weight without benefit. The FAQs are plain, crawlable HTML (`details`/`summary`).

## 5. Future editorial ideas (after launch, with original material)

1. **"A day in Tangier from Spain"**: a practical, photo-led guide for ferry visitors (timings, port, what to see). Target: Tangier day trip from Spain / from Tarifa.
2. **"Tangier for cruise passengers"**: port location, time budget, which tour fits which call length.
3. **"Chefchaouen in one day: an honest timeline"**: drive, lunch, viewpoints, when to leave.
4. **Seasonal notes**: Asilah arts festival (summer), Ramadan, best months for the coast.
5. **Guide portraits**: one short interview per guide, with photo. Builds trust and distinctive content marketplaces cannot copy.
6. **Market stories** for the food tour: what is in season at the fish market, a spice glossary.

Each piece should link to the relevant tour and destination pages and carry a single inquiry call to action. Avoid generic "top 10 things to do" lists copied from other sites.

## 6. Content that must not be published until verified

- Prices, "from" prices, discounts.
- Inclusions/exclusions beyond "private guide" and "private air-conditioned vehicle".
- Ratings, review counts, "5 stars", "TripAdvisor Excellence" badges.
- Founding year, years of experience, number of guests served.
- Licence numbers, insurance claims, "official guide" wording.
- A physical address or a map pin (the business currently publishes "Tangier, Morocco" only).
- Any Spanish or French page produced by machine translation without human review.

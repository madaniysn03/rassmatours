Visual identity

Introduction

Enjoy Morocco, Your Way

Rassma Tours reads like a boutique travel concierge, not a booking engine. Tangier is our home base; the journey reaches across Morocco, shaped around each traveller. The identity should convey three qualities at first glance.

01

Quiet luxury

Large photography, editorial serif type, generous white space, a restrained palette. No shiny gold, no ornament.

02

Human warmth

A first-person-plural voice, real places — cafés, markets, alleys — rather than postcard views.

03

Authenticity

Places named precisely, verifiable facts, no unsourced promises.

01 · The symbol

The Strait

The symbol starts from the khatam, the Moroccan eight-pointed star made of two squares rotated 45°, reinterpreted with restraint.

  • The solid star roots the brand in Moroccan heritage, without arabesques or decoration.
  • The channel splits the star from the western point (Atlantic) to the eastern point (Mediterranean) and narrows at the centre, like the Strait of Gibraltar off Tangier — the gateway to Morocco.
  • The sun, cut out in negative with a brass disc, rises above this horizon. It is the only touch of brass in the symbol.

A strong silhouette, legible in one colour and down to 16 px. Chosen from three routes: Zellige Horizon, The Strait, Bab.

10 · Photography and voice

Places lived in

Large images, full-bleed on opening screens, with a midnight gradient towards the bottom for legibility. Cafés, markets, alleys and the light of the Strait — from Tangier to Chefchaouen and Asilah — rather than generic views.

We write

  • “We”, at human scale
  • Named places: Petit Socco, Cinéma Rif, Café Baba, Cap Spartel, Chefchaouen
  • Verifiable public facts

We avoid

  • “Ultimate luxury experience”, “hidden gems”
  • Unverified review counts, unconfirmed prices
  • Shiny gold, mashrabiya screens and decorative arabesques
